# Calvin Boo Boo Ba Boo's Trip Home

A browser game made by Asa for Room 310. Calvin Boo Boo Ba Boo, a rockhopper penguin, got lost on the Eiffel Tower. Help him get all the way back to his penguin bag at school.

**Play:** https://asagames.github.io/calvin/

## How to play

- **Tablet or phone:** tap ◀ ▶ to waddle and the pink penguin foot to hop. Turn the screen sideways.
- **Computer:** arrow keys to waddle, space bar to hop.
- Follow the yellow arrow. Bumping into a bird only pushes Calvin back, and falling puts him back where he was, so you can't lose.
- Hop on pigeons and seagulls to make them fly away.

## The trip

1. Climb down the Eiffel Tower
2. Waddle through Paris to the Gare du Nord
3. Ride the train under the ocean to Boston
4. Walk from Maverick Station to school
5. Play on the playground: slides, the frog, the Tom-Tom drums
6. Climb the stairs to Room 310
7. Explore the classroom and find the penguin bag

## Sidequests

From the title screen, tap **Sidequests**:

- **Macarena Dance Party:** tap Dance! on the beat and Calvin does the moves (original music).
- **Trampoline Park:** tap Bounce! when the ring on the trampoline turns green. Every good tap adds bounce power; big bounces flip on their own.
- **Taekwondo Dojo:** tap Hi-yah! when the marker is in the green zone. Streaks multiply points, board stacks need several hits, golden boards are worth triple.

## Scoring

Score = fish × 100 + birds hopped × 50 + a bonus for being fast. There are 50 fish to find. High scores are saved only on the device you play on.
